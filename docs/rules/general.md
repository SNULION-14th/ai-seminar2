# General Rules

- Keep changes scoped to the requested task.
- Prefer simple solutions over premature abstractions.
- Do not add unnecessary dependencies.
- Do not introduce a backend, database, or server-side functionality.
- Never hardcode secrets, tokens, or environment-specific values.
- Remove dead code created or exposed by your changes.
- Preserve existing working behavior unless the spec requires otherwise.
- Do not consider a task complete until verification passes.
